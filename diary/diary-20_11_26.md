## Probe: slots as packaged and reworked, taught from the start on hands_off (development)

The scratch copy of yesterday's last entry (among chosen steps the one given the least
chance of being of the kind is let go, a step given less than every one kept is not kept,
steps chosen before the last judgement go first) beside the package's reader (`e401315`),
three slots, reach 12, seeds 6301 to 6316: four plays of 150 seconds at rung 1, four at
rung 2, eight at rung 3, 900 untaught steps after each rung, side by side with one other
process. Share of judgements paid, chance 0.25:

| | package | reworked |
|---|---|---|
| rung 1, untaught | 37 of 73 (0.507) | 37 of 73 (0.507) |
| rung 2, untaught | 9 of 48 (0.188) | 13 of 34 (0.382) |
| rung 3, while taught, eight plays | 89 of 1685 (0.053) | 165 of 1699 (0.097) |
| rung 3, untaught | 9 of 31 (0.290) | 4 of 38 (0.105) |

At rung 1 nothing is chosen yet in either and the two are the same reader. While taught at
rung 3, where about one trial in six outlasts the presses made at random, the reworked
reader was paid nearly twice as often. Its untaught test failed, below chance.

Why the test failed. After its last teaching the reworked reader gave the median unmarked
picture 0.65 and the median marked one 0.99. The network that says which steps to keep is
trusted only where the first is under half the second; it was not, so nothing was chosen,
and untaught, with no keeping at random, the slots stood empty at every question. The same
reader with that one figure set aside in scratch: 26 of 38 (0.684) on the test's seed and
22 of 39 (0.564) on another, 48 of 77 together. The package's reader of this run under the
reworked rule, nothing taught anew: 14 of 32 (0.438) against its own 9 of 31. So what was
learned under the reworked rule is the better reader, and the condition for trusting the
network is what let it down.

What gets marked. Of the pictures the two readers hold as tested, by whether they show the
red of the cue:

| | cue, marked | cue, unmarked | no cue, marked | no cue, unmarked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| package (175) | 46 | 12 | 95 | 22 |
| reworked (359) | 131 | 22 | 125 | 81 |

In both, pictures with no cue in them, the empty frames of the wait and the frame of the
last trial's outcome, are marked as often as the cue or more. In the reworked reader's
marks 0.86 of the cue's pictures are marked and 0.61 of the others, and a network fitted
to them can do no better than give those chances: enough to rank the two, which the
reworked rule needs, and not enough to put the others under a threshold of a half, which
the package's rule needs twice over (to choose a step, and to trust the network). The test that marks replaces every
place a picture holds in the row by the picture of now and marks it where the choice
changes. The empty frame of a trial fills up to eleven places of the buffer and a slot or
two; replaced in all of them at once the row is unlike any the reader was taught on, and
its choice changes for that reason.

Read on both points:

43. Yoon, Kim, Yang and Hwang 2018, Learning what to remember: long-term episodic memory
    networks (method and results in full, through a summary of the full text). A few slots
    over a long stream; a learned policy gives each held entry a retention value relative
    to the others and the entry let go is drawn by it, the incoming one among the
    candidates, so that a new entry can be refused. Trained by payment (A3C). Against
    first-in-first-out with five slots on bAbI: 17.5% errors against 41.4%; in mazes 73 to
    94% success against 60 to 91%. Taken: letting go by a value relative to what else is
    held, with refusal of the newcomer, is the known shape of the reworked rule, and
    first-in-first-out is their weak baseline. They have no threshold and no condition of
    trust; exploration is the drawing itself.
44. Sukhbaatar et al. 2021, Not all memories are created equal: learning to forget by
    expiring (method and experiments in full, through a summary of the full text). Each
    memory is given a learned span after which it is dropped; a memory dropped too early
    sends nothing back, and the paper has no means of recovering from that. Taken: a
    predictor of what to keep cannot correct itself on what it lets go, so keeping at
    random has to stay beside it; here that is the opening of chosen slots as often as
    judgements go unpaid, which makes the half-threshold on trust a second guard for the
    same danger.
45. Hooker, Mentch and Zhou 2019, Unrestricted permutation forces extrapolation (argument,
    simulations and alternatives, through a summary of the full text). Replacing a feature
    with a value that does not go with the others puts a flexible model where it has no
    data, and the change in its output is then credited to the feature; features that go
    together are overrated. Their remedy is to replace by a draw from what the feature
    could be given the rest. Taken: this is the marking of the empty frames. Tried
    offline, below.

harm of heavy early teaching that the survey of the 19th listed first among its findings

seen here. Shrinking the weights helps little; beginning the last part anew gets half
way; beginning everything anew is best and costs all the rounds again.
