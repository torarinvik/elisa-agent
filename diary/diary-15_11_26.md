## Where a port acts and what is paid there (development)

A second player, `places` in `nca-choices`, for tasks in which one press is judged. Nothing of the task is
told to it. It presses ports in turn to find which act; it takes a port to act where something comes to be
when the port is paid, apart from what comes to be on every trial; it places the other ports by taking
ports and places to run in rows alike; and a small convolutional net learns from payment what the frame
round a paid place looks like, seven steps of the lattice across, turned and mirrored freely. Where an
unpaid press is followed by a mark at another port's place, that port is taken as what would have paid.
