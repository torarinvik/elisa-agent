## Why one drawing or one order of a layout went far worse: development

On the seed 8128 puzzles, no longer fresh. Against the generator's records, every triangle of left and right
was read one size too large and its two largest sizes as one; every other shape was read right. The 150
pictures looked at held five stray patches of 4.6 pixels that lay nearest the triangle. A reach counted as
seen often when seen a quarter as often as the shape's most seen reach, and sightings of one reach are
counted to 20 at most, so five sufficed: the strays became the lowest rung of the triangle's ladder and moved
every size of it one up. A rung now counts only when the whole rung is seen a quarter as often as the shape's
most seen rung (`nca-choices` `b3e550a`).

Wrong of 300 on the same puzzles, before and after: left and right untaught 68 to 6, taught 56 and 54 to 4
and 3, in turn 57 and 57 to 5 and 5; up and down on order 52, taught 52 to 5, in turn 39 to 4. The seven
layouts together, of 2,100: in turn 186 and 214 to 130 and 123, taught 266 and 296 to 200 and 175, untaught
254 to 185. On seed 1234 untaught goes from 169 to 156. Which 150 pictures are looked at depends on the order,
so this is also why one order of a layout went far worse than another on seeds 2718 and 3141, which was
recorded then as not understood.
