### Joint choice of language teaching and timing

Results for the November 24 preregistration. Source 6144 randomized Karen installations, 4922 purchases, 6406.5 units in simulated fees. Training net 11619.5 vs 16000 for skipping (4380.5 source deficit). Across all eight held-out deployment conditions together the fitted policy recovered 4174 units vs skipping, leaving a cumulative deficit of 206.5. No empirical whole-study break-even. Each condition contains 64 paired simulated streams of 32 installations using recorded acoustic/meaning forks; the streams do not represent independent human speakers.

Mean net per installation in persistent/alternating regimes: Karen learner 2.778/2.704 vs skip 2.635/2.585, tuned partition-and-price rule 2.753/2.687, transition forecaster 2.748/2.713. Moira learner 3.257/3.214 vs skip 2.893/2.843, partition rule 3.187/3.121 and forecaster 3.288/3.252. Paired learner-minus-partition means and 95% intervals: Karen .024 [approximately 0,.049] / .017 [-.005,.039]; Moira .070 [.046,.095] / .094 [.071,.116]. None of the four point estimates met the preregistered +.1 margin vs partition rule. On unordered Moira persistent menus the learner selected 232 endpoints, 969 full paths and 847 skips out of 2048. Reversing menu presentation with the same public tie priorities preserved every selection. The learned weights were reconstructed independently by a dense fit on the chosen-action log. Hidden reversal: net learner/forecaster 2.708/2.647 Karen and 3.191/3.154 Moira. Under independent demand the partition heuristic slightly exceeded the learner on both voices.

One held-out candidate phrase also occurred in the rare future workload. Excluding this offer entirely, keeping all exogenous draws and fees for the remaining options, reduced learner-minus-skip to .060/.027 Karen and .259/.264 Moira in persistent/alternating streams. This changes the size and usefulness of the menu as well as removing direct phrase overlap.
