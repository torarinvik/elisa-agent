# Research diary, 17 November 2026

## Probe: seeking frames not yet seen does not reach a first payment (development, scratch only)

Yesterday's blind run left the question of how a first payment is reached where presses at random are
never paid. Tried the plainest answer that uses no payment and knows no task: reduce the frame to a
12 by 12 grid of coarse colours, remember which frame each press led to, press what is untried in the
frame at hand, else walk the remembered steps to the nearest frame with something untried. Against
presses at random, 30 seconds each, seed 220, rung 1, 27 development tasks (fog_of_war did not start).

| | random | seeking |
|---|---|---|
| tasks paid at least once | 10 | 8 |
| tasks paid by one and not the other | hidden_rule 1/20, heavier 2/132 | none |
| sokoban | 5/13 | 10/24 |
| crossings | 55/164 | 81/217 |
| graph_mapping | 102/212 | 146/282 |
| fold_it | 47/173 | 59/221 |
| n_in_a_row | 4/182 | 9/166 |
| wiring | 5/30 | 6/34 |

On the 17 tasks neither was paid on (maze, tower_of_hanoi, jigsaw, lander, crane, ferry, rail_yard,
you_are_here, firebreak, errands, salesman, sudoku, crossed_wires, chess, dispatch, scramble,
kitchen) seeking saw more distinct frames on about half (maze 75 against 26,
kitchen 51 against 17, tower_of_hanoi 181 against 121) and no more on the rest, and was judged no
more often.

Reading. Seeking new frames reached payment on no task where random presses did not. Where it is
ahead the task already pays random presses; the gain there is from not repeating a press in the same
frame. Covering more frames is not what stands between a player and its first payment on these
tasks: an end that is judged lies many presses deep and is one of very many frames, and nothing in
the count of visits points toward it. The frame signature is also crude (firebreak and jigsaw give
a new one at nearly every step, so nothing is ever revisited). Not built into the package.

## Probe: frames at paid judgements share no look across tasks (development, scratch only)

If the end that is judged cannot be found by covering frames, it might be told by its look, learned
on tasks that pay and carried to ones that do not. Probe: presses at random, 45 seconds, seed 221, on
the eight development tasks that pay them (sokoban, wiring, n_in_a_row, go_winning_move, crossings,
fold_it, graph_mapping, heavier). At each judgement the frame before the judging press and the frame
after it were measured as change from the episode's first frame, in six measures the learner's
seeing already gives: things, groups, kinds of shape, colour and size, how spread the things are,
and their area. Paid against unpaid judgements, differences beyond two standard errors:

- Before the judging press: sokoban alone in a way that reads as an end (kinds of shape 0.4 fewer
  when paid, 0.6 more when not; 10 paid, 11 unpaid), and wiring by 0.6 kinds of colour on six paid
  judgements. Nothing on the other six.
- After it: every task differs, mostly in area and kinds of shape (crossings 4.5 against 0.5 more
  kinds of shape, fold_it 0.9 more against 4.5 fewer, n_in_a_row 3.2 against 0.1). This is the mark
  the task draws to say right or wrong, not the state that earned it, and its sign is not the same
  from task to task.

Reading. With these measures there is no look of a paid end that several tasks share before the
judgement, so nothing here to carry to a task that pays nothing. Six of the eight judge one press, so
the frame before it is the question, not an end, and the question does not say which answer is paid;
only sokoban has an end reached over many presses, and one task is no ground for a shared look. The
mark after the judgement is told apart from payment easily on every task, which is no gain while
payment is given anyway. The probe is small and its measures coarse; it rules out the easy form of
the idea, not the idea.

On the other fourteen (heavier, hidden_rule, dr_shape, count, semaphore, numberology, gamble,

By yesterday's rule none passes. what_next is 2.1 standard errors above random (0.55 against 0.45)
and wiring is ahead on nine judgements; everywhere else the learner is level with random presses,
and on crossings and fold_it somewhat below. It followed readings on most of these (will_it_fall 200
of 411, who_knows_what 159 of 336) that paid no better than chance, so the guard against chance
readings held, and nothing better was among the readings.

Reading. The learner's mechanism, a reading found by payment and followed while it pays, is not what
limits it; the list of readings is. That list was written by hand from the five tasks it was shaped
on and reaches those, cookie_thief, and perhaps what_next. It is the same fault as a player per
task, one level down: a reading per kind of question. Tasks judged hundreds of times a minute
(crossings, graph_mapping, fold_it, point_of_view, will_it_fall) give enough payment to learn from,
and there the readings would have to be made, not listed.

## Probe: a reader taught by payment alone, from the pixels (development, scratch only)

If the readings must be made and not listed, the plainest maker is a network from the frame to the
ports, taught by payment. Presses at random for four minutes, seed 230, rung 1, on ten development
tasks, keeping each question's frame reduced to 96 by 96, the port pressed and whether it was paid
(only judgements of one press). A network of four convolutions and two layers gives a number per
port and is taught on the port pressed alone; 25 times over the data, 8 to 136 seconds on the
processor. Then it plays fresh questions for 60 seconds, seed 231, choosing among ports that were
ever judged.

| task | questions taught on | live, fresh seed | share paid to random presses |
|---|---|---|---|
| touching | 1482 | 869/869 | 0.35 |
| inside_or_out | 3191 | 812/812 | 0.49 |
| will_it_fall | 652 | 951/951 | 0.52 |
| who_knows_what | 1575 | 795/795 | 0.48 |
| what_next | 334 | 201/346 | 0.46 |
| crossings | 1123 | 371/858 | 0.32 |

Held back, not played live (the last quarter of the collected questions, counted where the
network's choice was the press made): graph_mapping 178/374 against 0.48, fold_it 32/173 against
0.23, point_of_view 40/160 against 0.30, syllogism 3/14: level with chance, though the network fits
the questions it was taught on.

Checks on the scores without error. No test picture is the same as one taught on. The frames of
will_it_fall, sorted by which answer was paid, show two blocks and two buttons and nothing that
names the answer; the answer goes with how far the upper block hangs over. The hand-written
readings were at chance on will_it_fall and who_knows_what and at 142/257 on touching.

A finding by the way: on most of these tasks only two or three of the ports are judged (crossings
three of seven, will_it_fall two of eight); the seeking learner and random presses spent most of
their presses on ports that do nothing.

Reading. With hundreds of questions, payment alone teaches a small network what the hand-written
readings could not hold, on four tasks without error, and with nothing written about any frame.
Where the answer needs counting or turning in the mind (crossings, fold_it, point_of_view) it is at
or little above chance at this number of questions.
