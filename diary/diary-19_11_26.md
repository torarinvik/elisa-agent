# Research diary, 19 November 2026

## Probe: three readers up short_maze on the same seeds, one with a count of presses to payment (development)

Read first, since yesterday's worth did not tell progress. Rank2Reward (Yang et al. 2024,
method section in full): progress is learned by ranking the frames within successful
trajectories by time; off those trajectories the ranking gives spurious high values, which
the paper damps with a classifier of the successes' states against the policy's own. SASR
(Ma et al. 2024, method section in full): a state is scored by the share of its occurrences
in successful trajectories, which is what yesterday's worth was, and the paper names the same
limit, that it ignores where in a trajectory a state lies. Taken: order within the paid
trials, not membership of them, as in Paul et al. read yesterday. Not taken: the classifier
against states never paid.

The count: for each picture of a paid trial, how many presses the trial still had to go,
learned as a class by a net of the reader's own shape from the reader's own paid trials. In
an unpaid trial the last press is taught unpaid; an earlier press is taught paid where the
picture after it is counted one press nearer payment than the picture before, and is
otherwise not taught. Nothing in it names a task.

Three readers from the one that held rung 1 of short_maze (9010 steps), three plays of 150
seconds on each of rungs 2 to 5, seeds 2621 to 2653, no play cut off: the package reader as
it is; the same taught whenever 600 new steps are held, or a tenth of those held if more;

At rung 5 together: 487/1418 (0.34) with the count, 304/1448 (0.21) taught oftener, 153/3224
(0.05) as it is. In the first 200 trials at rung 5: 61, 74, 80; 46, 39, 51; 5, 11, 5.

The teaching by new steps is most of it: the reader as it is was taught in 3 of its 12 plays
and thinned rung by rung as yesterday; taught once every play it held 0.16 to 0.43 at every
rung. It cost more: each teaching ran on after its play, and a play held 720 to 1008 trials
where the reader as it is, untaught, played 2075 to 2457 in the same 150 seconds.

The count fired where yesterday's worth did not. Of the earlier presses of unpaid trials it
taught as paid 0/11, 0/24, 14/32 at rung 2; 17/144, 17/123, 26/145 at rung 3; 89/299,
91/275, 86/263 at rung 4; 106/590, 74/544, 80/565 at rung 5. With it the reader went
further into a trial (2.9 to 3.1 presses a trial at rung 5 against 2.3 to 2.6) and was paid
more at rungs 4 and 5 in every play.

What it does not show: one climb each on one task, so the difference between the two taught
oftener is not yet told from the run; whether the gain is from the presses taught paid or
from the right presses before a wrong one being left untaught (yesterday's without a count
that fired was also ahead at rungs 3 and 4); and how often a press the count taught paid was
a right one. The readers begun anew at rung 5 and at random are not in it.
