### Diagnostic lesson search stopped at its feasibility gate

Exact historical kernel replay recovered both waiting and purchased-branch accuracy in 256/256 installations. With the original three previews buffered and the updater fixed, a payable choice existed in 96/128 attested and 127/128 withheld installations. The historical choice missed the best payable lesson in 2 and 95, respectively. On withheld installations, average incremental net was −1.286 for the historical choice, +0.043 for first disagreement and +4.538 for the best legal receipt in hindsight. Only 10/128 showed a difference between the inherited and flat readers' top answers on any offered follow-up at that checkpoint.

In the separate development benchmark (32 fresh acoustic lexicons per family, 48 crossed forks per lexicon), optimistic hindsight improved on cost-sensitive first disagreement by +0.331 net units in both families. No installation met the preregistered +0.50 threshold; eight per family were required. Public recurrence priority gained +0.076 and the public reply-model planner lost 0.094 versus first disagreement in both families. There were zero pairs with uniquely different payable purchases across the two histories. The Mac kernel reproduced 5,376 candidate valuations. The study stopped without fitting, expanding the menu, or claiming a reduction in target teaching. Reproduction instructions and raw evidence are in the external diagnostic-search archive.
