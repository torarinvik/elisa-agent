# Research diary, 18 November 2026

## The one payment on time_it is what pressing seldom gives (development)

Yesterday's learner of what each press is judged was paid once on time_it on seed 241. Four
more seeds, 1300 presses each: one payment on 242 (press 681) and on 245 (press 1118), none on
243 and 244; 45 to 54 judgements a seed. Presses at random with one press in two, same seeds:
no payment in 643 to 663 judgements a seed.

The comparison that was missing: the one port pressed at random one step in twenty-five and
nothing pressed otherwise, with no learning, 1300 presses, seeds 241 to 246. One payment on
243 (press 435) and on 245 (press 1012), none on the other four; 41 to 61 judgements a seed.

So the learner was paid 3 times in 252 judgements over five seeds and seldom pressing at
random 2 times in 287 over six: the same within what so few payments can tell. What the
learner learned is to press seldom, and the payments are what seldom pressing gives; it did
not learn when to press, and after its one payment it was not paid again. The sign on time_it
is withdrawn.

Standing on first payment by learning within a task: nothing on the twelve tasks that judge
often and never pay. Their judgements say only "punished", the same after every answer, and a
learner within the task has nothing else to learn from. The condition as written needs
something brought from other tasks, which is the goal's third condition, or tasks whose first
rungs are graded.

## Preregistration: old experts on the frames they were taught on, as the library grows

The chain results compare each expert's outputs on unseen frames. The goal's first condition
names frames seen in teaching as well, and that has not been claimed apart. The library left
by yesterday's chain through the core's joining (24 blocks, 24 experts) is copied; core
ecda5b9 and choices package ff6b47c, unchanged. For each of the 24 experts the outputs are
kept on the first 64 frames it was taught on (seed 300) and on the 128 unseen frames stored
with it. Then two experts are joined that the library has not held: what_next and crossings
turned upside down, 3000 random presses of seeds 300 to 302 (what_next 960), offered the two
blocks nearest its key and the two most read, each given no frame but its own task's. Then, in
a new process with the library reopened, the 24 outputs are taken again.

Passed when all 24 are the same to the last bit on the frames taught on and all 24 on the
unseen ones, the library holds 24 blocks and more after the joining, and no more than three
are held at once. A joining step cut off by the clock fails the claim. Prediction: passed, by
the construction theory 66.2 states.

## Result: old experts on frames taught on, as the library grows (passed)

Neither joining step was cut off. what_next upside down kept a reading beside what_next_v1 and
what_next_v0 (held-back loss 0.066 against 0.131 for its own block alone) and crossings upside
down one beside crossings_v0 and crossings_v1 (0.166 against 0.192); the library went from 24
blocks to 26. Reopened in a new process, all 24 earlier experts gave outputs the same to the
last bit on the 64 frames they were taught on, and all 24 on their 128 unseen frames. No more
than three blocks were held at once.
