# Research diary, 13 November 2026

## Joins and likenesses under one test: result

The preregistration is the last entry of 12 November. Snake pixels, fresh seeds 128 to 139. Both learners admitted
on all 12. As shipped, strict joins and no likeness: a median of 95.5 takeovers (24 to 217), 1190 in all. Joins and
likenesses under the one test: a median of 95 (33 to 136), 1012 in all, fewer on 8 seeds and more on 4; every seed
ended with the four directions in one group holding one mirror. Admission, the median without, the count of seeds
and the structure held; the median with likeness was predicted at 50 to 90. By the rule set beforehand, fewer on at
least 9 seeds, likeness stays off in Snake.

The one test does what it was made for: no action is left apart with likenesses of its own, where five of twelve
seeds ended so before. It does not make a mirror pay on Snake. The medians are equal, and the gain is in the
longest teachings only. With the four directions joined, a mirror can at most halve the classes of a view of two
to six probes; against that stand a pivot to be found and a view learned afresh whenever a join or likeness
changes. On Othello, with one action and all eight turns to gain from, the same mechanism took a quarter off the
takeovers.

## Looking for a block whose side is known (THEORY §54.11)

Under a budget of 512 pixels a frame few pixel agents passed the gate. Exploratory, tuning seeds 0 to 5, agents taught
on the whole screen and playing 3,000 moves alone under the budget: the food was unfound in 4.2% to 5.3% of frames,
and the person objected to 50 to 81 of the moves made there, about half, against 17 to 34 of the 2,850 made with the
food found. Each time the food was eaten, 120 looks went to rings around where it had been and the rest to a random
scatter, which finds nine pixels among 4,096 in about 410 looks on average.

`Gaze` now measures the side of each block when it first finds it. Its rings step by that side, 15 looks for 120
here, and a missing block is looked for at the positions whose coordinates are multiples of its side, 484 here,
before the scatter. The same agents and moves: the food unfound in 0 to 6 frames of 3,000, 18 to 30 objections in
all, 251 to 274 eaten where it was 234 to 259, and 84 to 91 pixels read a frame where it was 122 to 127. Agents taught
under the budget, tuning seeds 0 to 7: 7 of 8 admitted with 35 to 93 takeovers, the eighth not after 252. At a budget
of 128 the food stays unfound in 12% to 17% of frames: 484 positions are several frames of looks.

Preregistration, written before running. Fresh seeds 140 to 151, the scripted person, teaching to a limit of 5,000
moves and then 3,000 moves alone. Three arms: taught on the whole screen; taught under a budget of 512 with the
search as it was (the core at `c7a67dc`); taught under 512 with the new search. Kept if, with the new search, at
least 9 of 12 are admitted, the median takeovers are at most 150, the median eaten is at least 250, and fewer pixels
are read a frame than with the old search on every seed. Prediction: new search, 10 to 12 admitted, a median of 50 to
110 takeovers, 255 to 270 eaten, 85 to 95 pixels a frame; old search, 0 to 3 admitted and a median of 230 to 340
takeovers; whole screen, 11 or 12 admitted and a median of 60 to 110 takeovers. Also reported, not a criterion: the
new search taught under a budget of 256, predicted 4 to 10 admitted and a median of 235 to 258 eaten.

Every criterion holds: 12 admitted, a median of 45 takeovers, 267.5 eaten, and fewer pixels read than the old search
on every seed. Of the predictions, the admissions with the new and the old search, the eaten and the pixels held.
The takeovers did not: 45 with the new search where 50 to 110 was predicted, 423 with the old where 230 to 340 was,
and 55 on the whole screen where 60 to 110 was. At 256 none was admitted where 4 to 10 was predicted; the eaten, 236,
held. On ten of the twelve seeds the agent taught under 512 took exactly the takeovers of the one taught on the
whole screen: it is the same teaching, read through a fiftieth of the pixels.

At 256 the bound of 54.10 is what fails: 484 marked positions against about 230 looks to spare, so a food that jumps
is unfound for a frame or more about half the time, and the person objects there as before. The search is within
rounding of the least any order can do for a block of nine pixels; a smaller budget needs something other than a
better order, such as acting sensibly while the food is unfound, or knowing where food can appear.

## Where a block has been (THEORY §54.12)

At a budget of 256 the search of 54.11 cannot find a jumped food within the frame. Could the agent instead act well
without it? Exploratory, 60,000 moves of the scripted person's play, the frames in which the food has just moved:
the person objects to a random safe move in 46% of them, to keeping the heading in 55% and to the safe move nearest
the centre in 24%. No choice made without the food stands often enough for the gate, so the food must be found.

`Gaze` now also keeps, for each block, the greatest step dividing every displacement of its corner seen, and where
that is at least the block's side looks only at positions that step apart from its first corner: 256 here for 484.
A pass that misses sets the stride aside until the block is found. Exploratory, tuning seeds 0 to 7 taught under a
budget of 256: 5 of 8 admitted, with 43 to 178 takeovers, where none was; agents taught on the whole screen and
playing alone under 256 (seeds 0 to 3) had the food unfound in 18 to 38 frames of 3,000. Under 128, seeds 0 to 7:
none admitted.

Preregistration, written before running. Fresh seeds 152 to 163, the scripted person, a limit of 5,000 moves and
3,000 moves alone. Arms: taught under 256 with the core at `97caebc`; under 256 with the stride; under 512 with the
stride. Kept if under 256 at least 6 of 12 are admitted with a median of at least 250 eaten, and under 512 at least
11 are admitted. Prediction: 256 with the stride, 6 to 10 admitted, a median of 60 to 250 takeovers and 255 to 268
eaten; 256 before, none or one admitted and a median of 340 to 430 takeovers; 512, 11 or 12 admitted and a median of
35 to 90 takeovers.

Of the three criteria two hold, 7 admitted under 256 and 11 under 512, and one fails: the median eaten under 256 is
246 where at least 250 was required. By the rule set beforehand the change has not met its bar. Predictions: the
admissions in all three arms and the takeovers under 256, with and without the stride, held; the eaten under 256,
predicted at 255 to 268, and the takeovers under 512, predicted at 35 to 90, did not.

The stride is kept in the core all the same, for what it does measurably: under 256 it takes agents from none
admitted to seven, and it reads fewer pixels at both budgets. It is not shown to make a budget of 256 as good as
the whole screen: five agents are not admitted, and one of them eats 91. Under 512 the agent with the stride takes
the takeovers of the whole screen on ten of twelve seeds, and the one it does not admit, seed 159, is not admitted
on the whole screen either; these seeds teach harder than 140 to 151 in every arm.
