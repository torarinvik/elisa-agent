# Research diary, 12 November 2026

## Pong: a learner that reads every tick and decides every few

Design, from THEORY §52.11. The trainer in `nca-pong` now keeps one observation per tick in the history, decides every
three ticks and holds its action in between, and files the person's demonstrated keys per tick. The action marked at a
takeover is the one in force 0.25 seconds, five ticks, before the key press. The unjudged margin is two decisions, six
ticks. Admission plays fresh serves alone in the same way, with the state advanced every tick and the action changed at
decision ticks. The careful scripted teacher judges a decision against the ball when it was made; the sloppy one judges
every tick. The core learner is unchanged.

Tuning seeds 0 to 11, exploratory: careful 9 of 12, sloppy 11 of 12. The learner that read once per decision gave 7 and
6 on the same seeds.

Preregistration, written before running. Fresh seeds 36 to 47, never run before. Same teaching procedure: blocks of 100
serves, stop at admission, limit 800, admission by the certified gate on 300 fresh serves. The bar is unchanged: at
least 8 of 12 admitted with the careful teacher and at least 6 of 12 with the sloppy one. Both must hold for the
learner to count as ready for people to teach it. Prediction: careful 8 to 10, sloppy 9 to 12.

Result, fresh seeds 36 to 47. Careful 7 of 12 (admitted 36, 38, 39, 41, 42, 43, 45; not 37, 40, 44, 46, 47). Sloppy
9 of 12 (not 37, 44, 47). The sloppy teacher clears its bar and the careful one misses by one seed, so the learner is
not ready. The prediction was right for the sloppy teacher and wrong for the careful one.

Diagnostic, tuning seed 0, careful teacher, 300 serves, exploratory. The careful teacher holds the key it chose at a
decision for the whole step, while the trainer now files a demonstration at every tick. Of the demonstrations filed
with the ball level, 72 at decision ticks are all "stay", but between decisions 24 are "down" and 22 are "up", chosen
when the ball was still below or above. With the ball below, 3 "up" between decisions, none at decision ticks. The
fault §52.11 removed from the agent's own steps reappears in the person's: a demonstration is a decision made at one
tick and held, so filing it at the ticks after misfiles it whenever the ball crossed a band in between.

## Judgements of uncertain time

THEORY §52.12 corrects §52.11: a judgement concerns a tick the learner does not know, and a shown action held from
before the step concerns none of the step's ticks. Four ways of filing, compared on tuning seeds 0 to 11, exploratory.
The decision interval, margin, teachers and procedure are as above.

| Filing | Careful | Sloppy |
|---|---|---|
| Every tick (§52.11) | 9 | 11 |
| Every tick where the ball stayed in one band since the decision, shown and played alike | 8 | 11 |
| Shown actions only at decision ticks, played at every tick | 11 | 10 |
| Shown actions only at decision ticks, played where the ball stayed in one band | 11 | 9 |

Filing shown actions where the ball did not move still lost the careful teacher three seeds. That teacher, taking
over mid-step, holds the key it chose at the previous decision, so the assumption that a judgement concerns a tick of
the current step fails for it. Filed only at decision ticks, where both scripted teachers have just chosen, it recovers.
Leaving the learner's own changed steps unjudged as well changes nothing measurable, so the design keeps the simpler
rule and the core is unchanged.

Preregistration, written before running. Shown actions filed only at decision ticks, played steps at every tick.
Fresh seeds 48 to 59, never run before; seeds 36 to 47 were spent on the design above and are not reused. Same
procedure and the same bar: at least 8 of 12 careful and at least 6 of 12 sloppy, both required. Prediction: careful 9
to 11, sloppy 8 to 11.

Result, fresh seeds 48 to 59. Careful 8 of 12 (not 48, 50, 52, 55). Sloppy 9 of 12 (not 50, 53, 55). Both bars hold,
so by the preregistered criterion the learner is ready for people to teach it. Careful lies at the bar's edge and below
the predicted 9 to 11; sloppy lies in its predicted range. Four of the seven failures end with three states, one per
band, so the learner found the right shape and some label in it is wrong; seeds 52 and 55 with the sloppy teacher grew
to 84 and 53 states. Over the two fresh sets, the careful teacher's misses are the weaker side.

## A real person reacts later than the scripted ones

The first person to teach the new trainer reported that the agent moved down always. Steps the person let pass before
the unjudged margin of two decisions, 0.3 seconds, count as good, and a person who watches the paddle go the wrong way
for a second before taking over approves the wrong action at every one of those steps. Exploratory, tuning seeds 0 to
3, 200 serves of a careful scripted teacher reacting in 0.8 to 1.6 seconds: none admitted, and the machines answer
"up" with the ball below or level. Reacting in 0.25 to 0.55 seconds, 3 of 4.

Margin against reaction, careful teacher, tuning seeds 0 to 5, the usual procedure:

| Margin, decisions | Slow, 0.8–1.6 s | Fast, 0.25–0.55 s |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 0 of 6 | 5 of 6 |
| 10 | 5 of 6 | 5 of 6 |
| none of the learner's steps judged | 5 of 6 | 4 of 6 |

The trainer now uses ten decisions, 1.5 seconds. The preregistered pass on seeds 48 to 59 was at two decisions and does
not cover this setting, which has not been tested on fresh seeds.

Preregistration, written before running. The trainer with a margin of ten decisions, fresh seeds 60 to 71, never run
before. Same procedure. Primary bar, unchanged: at least 8 of 12 careful and at least 6 of 12 sloppy, both reacting in
0.25 to 0.55 seconds. Secondary arm, new: a careful teacher reacting in 0.8 to 1.6 seconds, with a bar of at least 6
of 12. Prediction: careful 8 to 11, sloppy 7 to 11, slow 7 to 11.

Result, fresh seeds 60 to 71. Careful 10 of 12 (not 63, 71). Sloppy 11 of 12 (not 63). Slow careful 11 of 12 (not 71).
All three bars hold, and every arm lies in its predicted range. Ten of the twelve slow runs and all admitted runs but
one were admitted after the first 100 serves. The margin of ten decisions replaces two as the tested setting.

## A faster paddle, and guessing instead of waiting

At the person's request the paddle moves twice as fast, 0.020 per tick, and where the learner has no action the agent
guesses one instead of waiting for a demonstration. Exploratory, tuning seeds, margin of ten decisions.

At the faster paddle one decision moves the paddle 0.06, the width of the level band, so the band was widened to
±0.06 as well. Moving toward the ball meets it on 300 of 300 serves at either width. Waiting, seeds 0 to 5: band ±0.03,
careful 4 of 6 and slow 5 of 6; band ±0.06, 5 and 5. The wider band is kept.

Seeds 0 to 11, band ±0.06:

| Where the learner has no action | Careful | Sloppy | Slow |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wait for a demonstration | 10 | not run | 11 |
| Guess anew at every decision | 8 | 11 | 8 |
| Guess once per observation and hold it for the serve | 11 | 12 | 12 |

A guess redrawn every 0.15 seconds rarely stays wrong for as long as a person takes to object, and every chance match
restarts the person's patience, so few guesses are taken over and an episode no one took over teaches nothing. A guess
held for the serve stays wrong until the person objects. The held guess is kept. None of this has been tested on fresh
seeds.

## Tetris taught by takeover through judgements that carry (THEORY §53)

The Tetris package ranked placements by weights over board features chosen by hand: lines, heights, holes and
roughness. It now has none. For each placement the agent sees the piece and rotation as a context and the cells around
where the piece would land, its box with a column to each side and the row below, as filled or empty, 16 cells padded.
The core's `Relevance` keeps, per context, every set of at most four cells that no two judgements contradict, 2517 sets,
and the agent proposes the placement with the widest good majority, or guesses. A person who takes over judges the
proposal bad and their own placement good. The scripted teacher takes over when the proposal leaves an empty cell under
the piece and some placement would not, and shows the lowest such placement. Admission: 300 fresh games of ten pieces
played alone, each without a hole another placement would have avoided, within the certificate.

Exploratory, tuning seeds. Counting an unopposed proposal as judged good, as passed steps are in Pong, taught nothing:
on seed 0, 240 takeovers in 600 pieces and no admission, with 14 of 19 contexts left without a consistent set. On 98 of
those 600 pieces no placement avoided a hole, the teacher let the proposal pass, and the pass contradicted the
judgements against holes: the teacher's verdict there depends on the rest of the board, outside the cells seen, which
is the limit §53.4 names. With unopposed proposals left unjudged, takeovers on seed 0 were 51 in the first 250 pieces and
14 in the next 1750, and the agent was admitted.

Seeds 0 to 11, blocks of 250 pieces, limit 2000: the attentive teacher 12 of 12 admitted, after 500 to 2000 pieces and
49 to 65 takeovers; a teacher who notices a bad proposal seven times in ten, 10 of 12, after 52 to 66 takeovers. The
bound of 53.4 for these sizes is 2533 per context, far above what was used.

Preregistration, written before running. Fresh seeds 12 to 23, the same procedure. Bar: at least 8 of 12 admitted with
the attentive teacher and at least 6 of 12 with the one attentive seven times in ten. Prediction: 11 to 12 and 8 to 11.

Result, fresh seeds 12 to 23. Attentive 11 of 12 (not 22), after 250 to 1750 pieces and 44 to 66 takeovers. Attentive
seven times in ten, 9 of 12 (not 12, 21, 22). Both bars hold and both lie in their predicted ranges. Across all 24
attentive runs, tuning and fresh, takeovers stayed between 44 and 66 however many pieces were played, so a person's
effort is set by the number of distinct situations that matter, not by the length of play.
