# Research diary, 12 November 2026

## Pong: a learner that reads every tick and decides every few

Design, from THEORY §52.11. The trainer in `nca-pong` now keeps one observation per tick in the history, decides every
three ticks and holds its action in between, and files the person's demonstrated keys per tick. The action marked at a
takeover is the one in force 0.25 seconds, five ticks, before the key press. The unjudged margin is two decisions, six
ticks. Admission plays fresh serves alone in the same way, with the state advanced every tick and the action changed at
decision ticks. The careful scripted teacher judges a decision against the ball when it was made; the sloppy one judges
every tick. The core learner is unchanged.

Tuning seeds 0 to 11, exploratory: careful 9 of 12, sloppy 11 of 12. The learner that read once per decision gave 7 and
6 on the same seeds.

Preregistration, written before running. Fresh seeds 36 to 47, never run before. Same teaching procedure: blocks of 100
serves, stop at admission, limit 800, admission by the certified gate on 300 fresh serves. The bar is unchanged: at
least 8 of 12 admitted with the careful teacher and at least 6 of 12 with the sloppy one. Both must hold for the
learner to count as ready for people to teach it. Prediction: careful 8 to 10, sloppy 9 to 12.

Result, fresh seeds 36 to 47. Careful 7 of 12 (admitted 36, 38, 39, 41, 42, 43, 45; not 37, 40, 44, 46, 47). Sloppy
9 of 12 (not 37, 44, 47). The sloppy teacher clears its bar and the careful one misses by one seed, so the learner is
not ready. The prediction was right for the sloppy teacher and wrong for the careful one.

Diagnostic, tuning seed 0, careful teacher, 300 serves, exploratory. The careful teacher holds the key it chose at a
decision for the whole step, while the trainer now files a demonstration at every tick. Of the demonstrations filed
with the ball level, 72 at decision ticks are all "stay", but between decisions 24 are "down" and 22 are "up", chosen
when the ball was still below or above. With the ball below, 3 "up" between decisions, none at decision ticks. The
fault §52.11 removed from the agent's own steps reappears in the person's: a demonstration is a decision made at one
tick and held, so filing it at the ticks after misfiles it whenever the ball crossed a band in between.

## Judgements of uncertain time

THEORY §52.12 corrects §52.11: a judgement concerns a tick the learner does not know, and a shown action held from
before the step concerns none of the step's ticks. Four ways of filing, compared on tuning seeds 0 to 11, exploratory.
The decision interval, margin, teachers and procedure are as above.

| Filing | Careful | Sloppy |
|---|---|---|
| Every tick (§52.11) | 9 | 11 |
| Every tick where the ball stayed in one band since the decision, shown and played alike | 8 | 11 |
| Shown actions only at decision ticks, played at every tick | 11 | 10 |
| Shown actions only at decision ticks, played where the ball stayed in one band | 11 | 9 |

Filing shown actions where the ball did not move still lost the careful teacher three seeds. That teacher, taking
over mid-step, holds the key it chose at the previous decision, so the assumption that a judgement concerns a tick of
the current step fails for it. Filed only at decision ticks, where both scripted teachers have just chosen, it recovers.
Leaving the learner's own changed steps unjudged as well changes nothing measurable, so the design keeps the simpler
rule and the core is unchanged.

Preregistration, written before running. Shown actions filed only at decision ticks, played steps at every tick.
Fresh seeds 48 to 59, never run before; seeds 36 to 47 were spent on the design above and are not reused. Same
procedure and the same bar: at least 8 of 12 careful and at least 6 of 12 sloppy, both required. Prediction: careful 9
to 11, sloppy 8 to 11.

Result, fresh seeds 48 to 59. Careful 8 of 12 (not 48, 50, 52, 55). Sloppy 9 of 12 (not 50, 53, 55). Both bars hold,
so by the preregistered criterion the learner is ready for people to teach it. Careful lies at the bar's edge and below
the predicted 9 to 11; sloppy lies in its predicted range. Four of the seven failures end with three states, one per
band, so the learner found the right shape and some label in it is wrong; seeds 52 and 55 with the sloppy teacher grew
to 84 and 53 states. Over the two fresh sets, the careful teacher's misses are the weaker side.
