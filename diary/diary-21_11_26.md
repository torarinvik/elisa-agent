# Research diary, 21 November 2026

## Things for places: three more tries on the task's frames, and where it is left

Carried on from last night on the same store of `which_moved` (1,484 lessons,
395 judged presses kept apart, an eye that is not taught, the whole scene
moved at random in teaching so that no place is learned by rote).

1. Each thing cut at its own size (the strong ink's middle four fifths,
   shown at 16 by 16), in place of likeness over three sizes. With no
   teaching, the thing that moved and four boxes are then found on 391 of the
   395 trials (238 before), and the box nearest the moved thing is the right
   port on 223 of them in plain points, 258 by the ink alone, 312 (0.80) by
   how nearly the untaught eye's readings point the same way. Taught: the
   reader stays at chance for some 1,400 batches, then fits 0.995 of its
   lessons and is right on 100 of 395 kept apart.
2. Likeness begun as that pointing-the-same-way, sharply (a tenfold weight):
   the same course, 111 of 395 kept apart at 0.67 of the lessons fitted.
3. Things cut where the screen is unlike the mean of the play's pictures, to
   leave out the writing that is always there: worse, the four boxes and one
   moved thing found on 149 of 395, since the boxes always hold some shape and
   so are usual.

So on the frames of the task the relation is there to be read (0.80 with no
teaching at all) and the reader does not find it in 1,484 lessons; what it
finds instead is the lessons one by one, though no place can tell them apart.
The header of the task shows the number of the trial, and a dozen cuts a
picture are writing: a key to each lesson by rote lies ready among the things.
On the made-up tasks, with no writing and likeness exact, the same reader
learned the same two relations to 0.97 and 0.99.

Left here, said plainly: not placed, the prediction of 0.60 FAILED, and the
written rules tried (cuts by ink, own size) are scratch only. What it would
take, in order: an eye taught without payment to tell the shapes of one colour
apart (the likeness is at 0.80 where the made-up task had 1.00); many more
lessons than a play of 18,000 steps gives, which the frame after can supply
only once the reading is there; and things chosen by what a paid choice rested
on, as the slots are, so that writing is not among them. It is the same wall
as last night's from the other side: the relation is easy for a reader that is
given the right things and hard to find among the wrong ones.

## No harm by choosing among readings: placed, and its test stated (capability 9)

The map found the memory learner at 0.70 and 0.59 where the reader of one
frame is paid every time, at the same 3,000 presses. The remedy is the
chooser the theory already has (chapter 71): a reader of more than one step
now holds the reading of one frame as well, a block and two last parts of its
own over the picture of now, taught on the same steps, and its four last parts
are chosen among as two were, by which foretold the newest payments best. By
71.1 the part chosen foretold the scored presses at least as well as the
reading of one frame did; 71.5 says what cannot be promised of the presses to
come. Checks so far: a reader kept before it had that reading reads as it did
(a kept reader with the reading taken out of its file gives the same shares
for its two parts, bit for bit), and the three stored readers give the digests
they gave before. In the working tree of the package, not yet committed: its
other tests are running.

Test: the new learner on `match_shapes`, `odd_shape` and `next_shape`, 3,000

## Test stated before its run: exploring by how new the picture is to a port

Last night's draw by the reader's shares kept the waits and never pressed at
the question. The draw is now made to turn on what the shares cannot tell:
how often a press of that port has been judged at a picture like this one.
The picture is made small (12 by 12, its mean taken off, of unit length) and
for each port a ridge of one is kept over the pictures at which a press of it
was judged; the width of the picture of now against that ridge is 1 where no
like picture was met and 1 / (1 + n) where the same was met n times (Zahavy et
al., 2018, with the picture itself for the features). When a press is to be
made at random each port is drawn with its width for weight and pressing
nothing with weight one. At a picture where every port has been judged often
the draw is nearly always to wait; at one that is new to a port it is as even
as it was. A written rule, said plainly: the small picture and the ridge of
one are chosen by hand; features the reader learns are the replacement to be
tried.

Test: `newest_counts`, rung 1, the memory learner (package `47940d5`), 3,000
presses from nothing on the map's seeds. Prediction: after the first teaching
a trial lasts 30 presses or more (12 with even draws, 20 by shares); 0.10 or
more of the judgements after the first play are paid (0.012 to 0.036, and
none); of the draws made, one in twenty or more is a port. It fails if the
picture at the question is no newer to the ridge than the pictures of the
wait.
