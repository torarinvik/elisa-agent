# Theory

Mathematical foundations of the agent: composing modules, planning routes,
answering selectively under a risk target, and decoding by the input format.
Theorems are numbered by section.

This is the theory index. Chapters are organized by topic rather than date;
section and theorem numbers remain stable across files. Follow a chapter
link for its definitions, arguments, and results.
